## Track record, stated plainly

900+ marketplace feedback entries between 2002 and 2022 for packed-well,
as-described shipments. That history covers physical goods, not consulting
work, so it lives here as seller reliability — not as proof of AI results.

### Consulting proof policy

No client logos, quotes, or percentage claims appear on this site until
each one is named, linked, and measured from a real engagement. Placeholders
use an em dash plus a label (metric to confirm), never an invented number.
