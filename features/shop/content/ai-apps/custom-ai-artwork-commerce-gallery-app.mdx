### How it works

Four guided steps after checkout — minutes, not weeks:

1. **Define your direction** — Set the creative direction for your gallery, including the artistic approach and available generation settings.
2. **Choose your model** — Select from the image models available in your plan. Creator includes the core model; Studio and Pro include the full catalog including FLUX.2 Klein.
3. **Generate and curate** — Generate artwork on a schedule and review the results. Publish the pieces you want in your public gallery. Unpublished work stays private.
4. **Publish and sell** — Launch at your own subdomain, set prices, and let buyers purchase high-resolution digital downloads through the integrated storefront.

### Selling and fees

Every plan includes selling. Connect your own Stripe account during setup, publish artwork with a price, and buyers check out securely to receive a high-resolution download.

Artwork sales carry a 30% platform fee — you receive 70%. For a $20 sale: you receive $14.00, platform fee $6.00 (computed to the cent, before any applicable taxes or other charges the checkout may apply). No sales, no fees. A gallery is a place to publish and sell — not a guarantee of sales.

### Publishing controls

Generation is scheduled. Publication is human. That separation is the point: review the output, publish the work you want to show, and unpublished work stays private. Published work gets individual artwork pages with SEO and sitemap support.

### Frequently asked questions

**What is a custom art gallery?**

A dedicated online gallery for generative artwork, with its own `you.synthetic.pics` subdomain, creative direction, public gallery, and optional sales of high-resolution digital downloads.

**Do I need to know how to code?**

No. Setup is guided: after checkout you complete four steps — Direction, Model, Domain, Monetize. No programming required.

**Can I sell my artwork?**

Yes. Every plan includes a digital storefront. Connect your own Stripe account during setup, publish artwork with a price, and buyers check out securely. You keep 70% of each completed sale; a 30% platform fee applies. Payouts land in your connected Stripe account.

**Which image models are available?**

Creator includes the core gallery model. Studio and Pro include the full currently supported catalog, including FLUX.2 Klein. The setup wizard only offers models your plan includes.

**What happens to unpublished artwork?**

Unpublished work stays private. Only work you choose to publish appears in your public gallery with its own artwork page.

**Can I use my own domain?**

Not yet. Custom galleries use a `you.synthetic.pics` subdomain. Pro members receive first access when custom domains are introduced.

**Can I cancel my subscription?**

Yes — anytime, self-serve from your dashboard billing page. Your subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period.

**What happens when I use all my generations?**

Each plan includes a monthly allowance (100 Creator, 500 Studio, 2000 Pro) that renews each billing cycle. Unused generations do not roll over. Upgrade from dashboard billing if you hit your limit.
