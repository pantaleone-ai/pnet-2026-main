[AgentDNA](https://agentdna.ai/) is an agent registry with signed identity per agent, a policy gateway at runtime, and lifecycle management through CI/CD.

Every agent gets a signed credential. The registry handles versioning, the gateway enforces policy at runtime, and releases go through CI/CD.
