Most enterprise AI programs fail on architecture, not models.

Teams bolt agents onto workflows that were already broken. The demo works. Production does not. The gap is rarely intelligence. It is integration, data access, and handoff points nobody mapped.

Fix the foundation first. Define where the agent can execute without approval and where it must stop. Instrument every run. Measure cost per completed task, not demos shipped.

Everyone is focused on model choice. The real problem is the operating model underneath it.
